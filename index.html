Privacy Policy

Elite Equestrian Championships · Effective 8 November 2026

In short: Elite Equestrian Championships does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Your game progress stays on your own device.

This policy explains how the game Elite Equestrian Championships ("the app"), made by Omid Safa ("we"), handles information. It applies to the app on Apple and Android devices.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for or collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any other personal information. It has no accounts or sign-in.

Information saved on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • game progress, such as horses, coins, ribbons, and results
  • the rider name you type in (optional; it is never sent anywhere)
  • game settings, such as sound and music choices

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes it from your device.

No ads, tracking, or analytics

The app shows no advertising and contains no analytics, tracking, or third-party data-collection tools. It does not track you across other apps or websites. It works fully offline.

In-game coins

Coins are earned by playing and can only be spent inside the game. The app has no in-app purchases and does not handle payment information.

Links to music stores

The game's music is by the artist StormGurl. Inside the app, a "StormGurl Music" page offers links to the songs on Apple Music / iTunes and Spotify. These links are behind a parent check and open outside the app. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we do not receive any information from them.

Children

The app is designed to be suitable for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. This is consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
